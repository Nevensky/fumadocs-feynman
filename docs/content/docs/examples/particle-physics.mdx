## e⁻e⁺ → μ⁻μ⁺ through a photon or a Z

Time runs left to right. The electron comes in with its arrow towards the vertex, the positron with its arrow pointing back out, and the muon pair leaves the same way. prop= sets the line in the middle.

```feynman source
s-channel
caption "photon"
| s-channel prop=boson label="Z^0"
caption "Z boson"
```

## Møller scattering: the t and u channels

Two electrons exchange a photon. The outgoing electrons cannot be told apart, so the u-channel diagram, with the two swapped, comes with a relative minus sign.

```feynman source
t-channel in2="e^-" out2="e^-"
- u-channel
```

## Bhabha scattering: the t and s channels

anti makes the lower line of the t-channel diagram a positron, its arrows running right to left.

```feynman source
t-channel anti in2="e^+" out2="e^+"
- s-channel out1="e^-" out2="e^+"
```

## Compton scattering

The electron absorbs the photon and then emits one, or, crossed, emits first. The two photons then cross over the electron line.

```feynman source
compton
+ compton crossed
```

## Pair annihilation into two photons

The electron line runs from the incoming electron to the incoming positron, giving off a photon at each vertex. crossed swaps the photons.

```feynman source
annihilation
+ annihilation crossed
```

## The QED loops at one loop

These come from the many-body templates with a photon in place of the phonon or the Coulomb line: the electron self-energy, the vacuum polarisation and the vertex correction.

```feynman source
fan-migdal phonon=photon G="e^-" F="\gamma"
caption "electron self-energy"
| bubble ext=photon top="e^-" bottom="e^+"
caption "vacuum polarisation"
| vertex-correction ext=photon rung=photon
caption "vertex correction"
```

## The gluon self-energy: gluon, ghost and quark loops

bubble with line=gluon, line=ghost or line=fermion. The ghost loop carries arrows, as ghosts are fermions.

```feynman source
bubble ext=gluon line=gluon
+ bubble ext=gluon line=ghost
+ bubble ext=gluon line=fermion top=q bottom="\bar{q}"
```

## Triangle loops: H → γγ and gluon fusion

The Higgs boson decays to two photons through a loop of top quarks. fusion turns the triangle round: two gluons come in and make the Higgs.

```feynman source
triangle-loop
caption "H \to \gamma\gamma"
| triangle-loop fusion legs=gluon top=g bottom=g
caption "gg \to H"
```

## Light-by-light scattering

Four photons on a loop of four electron propagators: γγ → γγ, which classical electrodynamics forbids.

```feynman source
box-loop loopLabel="e^-"
```
