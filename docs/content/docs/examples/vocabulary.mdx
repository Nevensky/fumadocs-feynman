## Line styles

Built-in styles. `dressed` turns a style into its dressed form: double for fermion, scalar and plain lines, the screened line for coulomb, and heavy for phonon, photon, field, gluon and boson. Screened, ghost and impurity lines have no dressed form.

```feynman source
(0,0) -> (2.2,0) fermion "fermion"
(3.2,0) -> (5.4,0) fermion2 "fermion2"
(0,-1) -- (2.2,-1) phonon "phonon"
(3.2,-1) -- (5.4,-1) phonon2 "phonon2"
(0,-2) -- (2.2,-2) photon "photon"
(3.2,-2) -- (5.4,-2) photon2 "photon2"
(0,-3) -- (2.2,-3) coulomb "coulomb"
(3.2,-3) -- (5.4,-3) screened "screened"
(0,-4) -- (2.2,-4) field "field"
(3.2,-4) -- (5.4,-4) gluon "gluon"
(0,-5) -- (2.2,-5) scalar "scalar"
(3.2,-5) -> (5.4,-5) ghost "ghost"
```

## Vertex shapes

Lines stop at the edge of disks, blobs and boxes on their own.

```feynman source
a at (0, 0) dot "dot"
b at (1.1, 0) current "current"
c at (2.2, 0) disk "disk"
d at (3.3, 0) hatched "hatched"
e at (4.4, 0) open "open"
f at (5.5, 0) cross "cross"
g at (0.4, -1.5) blob label="\Sigma"
h at (2, -1.5) bubble label="\chi"
i at (3.6, -1.5) box label="\Gamma"
j at (5.2, -1.5) triangle label="\Lambda" dir=left
k at (0, -3) charge "charge"
l at (1.1, -3) eph "eph"
m at (2.2, -3) dipole "dipole"
n at (3.3, -3) spin "spin"
o at (4.8, -3) lens label="\chi" dir=right
```

## Vertices with their matrix elements

Any vertex can be labelled once it exists, a template’s too: write its name, then a label and where it goes (pos=), a shape or a colour. The names are on the By hand tab, and grid shows them. Charge vertices carry ρ, current vertices j; eph, dipole and spin are the other couplings, each in its particle’s hue.

```feynman source
bubble vertex=charge ext=coulomb extLabel=v
a0 "\rho_{n\mathbf{k},m\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{q}}(\mathbf{G})" pos=sw
b0 "\rho^*_{n\mathbf{k},m\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{q}}(\mathbf{G}')" pos=se
| bubble vertex=current ext=photon extLabel=D
a0 "j_{n\mathbf{k},m\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{q}}(\mathbf{G})" pos=sw
b0 "j^*_{n\mathbf{k},m\mathbf{k}+\mathbf{q}}(\mathbf{G}')" pos=se
```

## Drafting grid

A line that says grid draws the unit grid and the name of every vertex. Positions are (x, y) in units of 40 px, y up; the main line sits on y = 0, where an equation sets = and +. Delete the line when the diagram is done.

```feynman source
grid
a at (0, 0) disk; b at (2.8, 0) disk
leg a left; leg b right
b -> a fermion dressed "G" below
a -- b phonon dressed bulge=1.2 above "F"
```
